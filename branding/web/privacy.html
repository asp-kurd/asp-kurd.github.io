Privacy Policy (English)

ASP stores only what the app needs: your name, age, city and gender (entered by you), your email if you sign in with email, Google or Facebook (we receive only your name and email), your learning progress, and voice recordings you choose to save.

We use this data only to run the app, keep your progress safe and send learning reminders. We never sell or share it. Data is stored with Supabase.

Data deletion

You can request deletion of all your data at any time via the app or by contacting ASP through asp-poya.com. Data is deleted within 30 days.
